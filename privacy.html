Video Saver TTFB - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Video Saver TTFB handles information when users access its Android video-saving features.

2. Video Links and Downloads

When users submit video links, the application processes those links to retrieve supported videos. Downloaded videos may be saved locally on the user's device, including the device Gallery.

3. TikTok Login

If TikTok Login is enabled, users may authorize the application through TikTok's official authentication process. The app will request only permissions necessary for its supported features. Users can manage or revoke authorization through their TikTok account settings.

4. Device Storage

The application uses device storage to save and manage downloaded videos. Users may remove saved videos from their device.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party services, including TikTok and Facebook, to process user-provided links. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We aim to protect information handled by the application using appropriate technical measures. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable laws and relevant third-party platform rules.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in application functionality or legal requirements. Updates will be posted here.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the developer through the official application support channel.
